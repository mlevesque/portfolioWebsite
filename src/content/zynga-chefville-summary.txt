I led the design and implementation of <b>Design Mode</b>, a core gameplay feature that allowed players to move and place objects on the game’s isometric grid. My work also included <b>NPC behaviors and pathfinding</b>, <b>progression systems</b> for unlocking cooking stations, and <b>game logic</b> spanning both the frontend and PHP backend, including player data synchronization and verification. I also contributed to internal content tools that enabled designers and artists to add new decorations and cooking stations to the game.
