I primarily worked on the main hub used for navigating between slots games and spinning the wheel. In <b>Unity 3D</b> and <b>C#</b>, I implemented the main wheel functionality that would spin the wheel and land on a specific wedge using <b>time-independent rotational kinematics</b>, a <b>choreography system</b> for timing and executing complex animations and event triggers, and a scrolling feature on 3D objects with sping back behavior at the scroll limits.
