As part of the <b>Native Video Rendering</b> team, I developed video rendering features for Microsoft Teams in <b>C++</b>. My work included building a new cross-platform rendering pipeline designed for modularity and extensibility across <b>Windows</b> and <b>macOS</b>, as well as <b>performance optimizations</b> that reduced redundant buffer clearing and frame rendering. I also used <b>Copilot</b> and <b>AI agentic coding</b> workflows to rapidly build a <b>cross-platform benchmarking tool</b> for collecting and analyzing Teams performance data.
